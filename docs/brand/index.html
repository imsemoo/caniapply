Brand sheet. The square brackets are the scope of a job post. The square dot is you. Inside: you can apply. Outside: you can't, and here is the sentence that says so.

The idea

“Remote” often doesn't include your country. CanIApply finds the one sentence that decides, and shows it as proof.

The mark says exactly that: a scope, and whether you are inside it.

Rules

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least one bracket stroke (6 of 64 units) — use the mark's own tile as the box.
  • Do not redraw, rotate, round the corners of, or add effects to the mark. No gradients, glass or shadows.
  • Write the name as CanIApply in text. The lower-case caniapply is only the drawn wordmark.
  • Under 24 px, use mark-small.svg; it is drawn on a pixel grid.
  • Images for social posts carry no Arabic text.
